Privacy Policy

EditinPDF collects no data about you or your documents.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

EditinPDF is made by Consultin AI Pty Ltd, Australia. Contact: info@consultin.ai.

What the app collects

Nothing. EditinPDF has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers. The app does not connect to the internet.

Your documents

  • The PDFs you open and edit stay where you keep them: on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in another storage app you choose in the Files app. EditinPDF reads and writes them only there.
  • While you edit, the app keeps a recovery copy of unsaved changes on your device, so you can get your work back if the app closes unexpectedly. It is not included in device backups, and it is deleted when you save or discard, or after 30 days.
  • The app never sends your documents, their contents or their names anywhere.

Send Feedback

If you choose Send Feedback, the app opens an email in your own mail app, addressed to info@consultin.ai. It includes the app's version, your system version and your device model, so we can help you. It includes nothing from your documents. You can read and change the email before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use your email only to reply to you, and we do not share it.

Children

EditinPDF collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
