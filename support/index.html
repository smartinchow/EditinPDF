Common questions

How do I keep my changes?

Tap Save, or tap Back and choose Save. Choose Save As to keep the original and save your changes as a new file. Choose Discard to leave the file as it was.

The app closed before I saved. Is my work lost?

Usually not. Open the same document again and EditinPDF offers to restore your unsaved changes.

Why can't I edit a document?

Some PDFs are protected by their author, and some signed PDFs can't be changed without breaking the signature. EditinPDF opens those read-only.

Where are my documents?

Where you keep them: on your device, in iCloud Drive, or in another storage app in the Files app. EditinPDF does not move or upload them.
